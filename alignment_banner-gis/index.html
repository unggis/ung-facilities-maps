About This Dashboard — click to expand

This page reads the output of GIS_Banner_Alignment_Check.py, which compares every UNG building's Banner Action List against its GIS floor plan record. SF Alignment is the share of total GIS room square footage that matches Banner's reported square footage — weighted by area, so a handful of large buildings dominate the number. Building Score is the plainer count: the share of in-scope buildings with zero open ADD/REMOVE/UPDATE actions.

Some buildings are handled specially before scoring: known-noise buildings have symmetric ADD/REMOVE counts traced to a room-numbering convention mismatch and are excluded until resolved; Banner-only buildings have no GIS polygon and only REMOVE actions, almost always a retired building still in Banner; institutional splits are buildings where GIS and Banner deliberately code the same physical space differently (e.g. Cumming's J378/UC0124 pairing), and both sides count as clean.

Companion to the ad_gis_pipeline placement-rate dashboard — that one tracks whether people are mapped to the right room; this one tracks whether the room itself is described the same way in both systems.
